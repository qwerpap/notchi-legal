How to Keep a Ringing-Ears Diary and Share It With Your Doctor

Updated November 2026 · By the Notchi developer

Short answer. Mark one answer a day in Notchi's journal, then export the PDF from the journal screen and share it. The PDF lists your days of notes, your matched tone and minutes of sound. Exporting the full history needs Pro; the last 7 days are free to view. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

Step by step

  1. Open the Journal tab and mark todayAnswer how the ringing felt today and add an optional note.
  2. Turn on the evening reminderChoose a time in Settings so you do not forget to mark the day.
  3. Check the 30-day viewBars show minutes of sound per day, and a streak counts consecutive days.
  4. Read the what-helps cardAfter a week or two of marks, Pro compares days with and without your sound.
  5. Export the PDFTap Export PDF, wait for Your PDF is ready, and tap Share PDF to send it or save it.

Frequently asked questions

What does the PDF contain?

Your days of notes, your matched tone and minutes of sound.

Is the journal free?

The last 7 days are free. Older days, the what-helps view and the PDF need Pro.

Can the journal prove that sound helps?

No. It shows patterns in your own marks. Interpret them with a clinician.

Can I add notes?

Yes, an optional note each day.

Is Notchi a medical device or a treatment?

No. Notchi is a wellness app that plays relief sounds and helps you keep a journal. It does not diagnose, cure or treat any condition, and it is not a hearing test. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

Does my journal leave my phone?

The journal is stored on your device. Nothing is shared unless you export a PDF and send it yourself.
