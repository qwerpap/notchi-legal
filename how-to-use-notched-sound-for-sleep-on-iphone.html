How to Listen to Notched Sound for Sleep on iPhone

Updated November 2026 · By the Notchi developer

Short answer. Match your tone first, then play a soft sound in Notchi: it removes a one-octave band around your tone from pink, white, brown, rain, ocean, fan or a low hum. Keep the volume gentle, use headphones and set a sleep timer. Notched sound is a technique explored in some small studies; Notchi presents it as a relief sound, not a treatment. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

Step by step

  1. Match your toneRun the two-minute calibration so Notchi knows which band to cut out.
  2. Open Listen and choose a soundPick one of seven sounds: pink, white, brown, rain, ocean, fan or a low hum. Each plays with your tone cut out.
  3. Set a gentle volumeKeep it comfortable and below your ringing. Louder is not better.
  4. Set the sleep timerChoose 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes. The sound fades out over 30 seconds and keeps playing with the screen off.
  5. Mark how the day feltOpen the journal next morning and record how the ringing felt, so you can see what helps.

Frequently asked questions

How long should I listen each day?

There is no official number. Many people start with 15 to 30 minutes and use the journal to see what feels better. Ask your audiologist or doctor for advice about your case.

What is notched sound?

Noise with a narrow band of frequencies around your matched tone removed. Notchi cuts one octave.

Will it work with the screen off?

Yes. Playback continues with the screen locked, and the sleep timer fades it out.

Does it cure ringing in the ears?

No. Notchi does not claim to cure or treat anything; it offers relief sounds for comfort.

Do I need headphones?

Yes, wired or wireless headphones work best. The calibration plays a quiet tone and the notched sound is meant to be listened to close to the ear at a gentle volume.

Is Notchi free?

The calibration, a 10-second sample of each sound and the last 7 days of the journal are free. Keeping a sound playing, older journal days and the PDF export need Pro: $9.99/month (3-day free trial), $49.99/year (7-day free trial) or $79.99 lifetime in the US.
