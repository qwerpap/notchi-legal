Notchi is a wellness app. Tone matching is not a hearing test, and notched sound is not a treatment, a cure or a medical device. It does not replace a doctor, an audiologist or a hearing aid. If your ringing is new, in one ear, pulsing, or comes with hearing loss, pain or dizziness, see a doctor. Keep the volume low — softer than your ringing.
