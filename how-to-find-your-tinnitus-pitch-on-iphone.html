How to Find the Pitch of Your Ringing Ears on iPhone (Tone Matching)

Updated November 2026 · By the Notchi developer

Short answer. Use headphones and a tone-matching exercise: start quiet, slide a tone until it sounds like your ring, choose the nearest octave, then fine-tune. Notchi does this in about two minutes for free and saves the tone for your notched sound. It is a sound-matching exercise, not a hearing test. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

Step by step

  1. Put on headphones and set a gentle volumeStart with the volume low and raise it only until you hear the tone clearly. It should stay softer than your ringing.
  2. Move the tone to your ringSlide the pitch control until the tone sounds like the one you hear. Close is enough; the next step checks it.
  3. Pick the closest octaveNotchi plays three tones an octave apart, because octaves are easy to mix up. Tap each one and choose the nearest.
  4. Fine-tuneMake small nudges and stop when the tone blends with your ring.
  5. Save your toneTap This is my tone. Notchi stores it and uses it to cut a one-octave band out of your sounds.

Frequently asked questions

How long does tone matching take?

About two minutes. You can repeat it any time if the sound you hear changes.

What if I hear more than one sound?

Match the one that bothers you most. You can run the exercise again for another pitch.

What if my ringing is more like hiss than a tone?

Notchi can use narrow noise instead of a pure tone for matching, and the saved tone shows which one you used.

Is the result exact?

No. It is a close match by ear, good enough to place the notch. It is not a diagnostic measurement.

Is Notchi a medical device or a treatment?

No. Notchi is a wellness app that plays relief sounds and helps you keep a journal. It does not diagnose, cure or treat any condition, and it is not a hearing test. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

Do I need headphones?

Yes, wired or wireless headphones work best. The calibration plays a quiet tone and the notched sound is meant to be listened to close to the ear at a gentle volume.

Does my journal leave my phone?

The journal is stored on your device. Nothing is shared unless you export a PDF and send it yourself.
