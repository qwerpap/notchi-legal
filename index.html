Ringing Ears, Calm Nights

Notchi is an iPhone app of relief sounds for people who live with ringing in the ears. You match a tone to the pitch you hear, then listen to soft noise with a one-octave band around that tone removed (a notched sound), and keep a short daily journal of how the ringing felt. It is a wellness tool for comfort and sleep, not a treatment. Notchi is not a medical device; talk to an audiologist or doctor about ringing in your ears.

What Notchi does

  • Tone matchingTwo-minute calibration with headphones: set a gentle volume, slide a tone to your ring, pick the closest of three octaves, then fine-tune.
  • Pink noise with your tone cut outSoft noise with a one-octave band around your tone removed, in seven sounds: pink, white, brown, rain, ocean, fan and a low hum.
  • Your journal for the last 7 daysA daily check-in (how did the ringing feel today, plus an optional note), a 30-day view with minutes of sound per day and a streak.
  • An evening reminder to mark your dayAn optional evening reminder, set at a time you choose, to mark your day.
  • Seven sounds, each with your tone cut out: pink, white, brown, rain, ocean, fan and a low humThe same notched treatment applies to every one of the seven sounds, so you can pick the one that is comfortable to fall asleep to.
  • Sleep timer that fades out, and playback with the screen offA sleep timer (15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes) that fades out over 30 seconds, and playback that continues with the screen off.
