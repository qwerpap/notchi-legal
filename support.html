Common questions

How do I match my tone?

Put on headphones in a quiet room, open My tone → Match again, keep the volume low, move the tone until it sounds like your ring, then pick the closest of three octave options and fine-tune.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Notchi → Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the paid period.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings in Notchi → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Is this a treatment?

No. Notchi is a wellness sound app, not a medical device. Please see a doctor about your tinnitus.
