Privacy Policy

Notchi: Tinnitus Relief Sounds · Last updated September 27, 2026

Notchi is a wellness sound app: you match the tone you hear, listen to noise with that tone cut out, and keep a short daily journal. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no server that stores your data, and we don't sell or share it.

What stays on your device

  • Your matched tone (a frequency and a volume) and the date you matched it.
  • Your answers to the first-run questions (how long, what it sounds like, when it bothers you most).
  • Journal marks (quieter / same / louder), the optional notes you tick, and minutes you listened.

This is self-reported wellness information you choose to note. It is not sent anywhere and Notchi does not use HealthKit.

Sound

All sounds are generated on your iPhone. Notchi does not use the microphone and never records audio.

PDF export

If you export your diary, the PDF is created on your iPhone and goes only where you share it.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store. Notchi receives only the status of your purchase, never your payment details. We use a subscription-management service (Adapty) that receives an anonymous app-install identifier, your purchase status and which onboarding step was reached, to validate purchases and measure how the paywall performs. Your answers, your tone and your journal are never sent.

Notifications

If you allow them, Notchi schedules local reminders on your iPhone: one a day before a free trial ends, and an optional daily journal nudge. No push server is involved.

Analytics and tracking

Notchi does not track you across apps or websites, does not use advertising identifiers and contains no third-party analytics beyond the subscription service above.

Deleting your data

Delete Notchi from your iPhone to remove everything it stored.

Children

Notchi is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
